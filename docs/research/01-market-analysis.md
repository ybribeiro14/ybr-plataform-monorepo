### Critical Trends in 2026

1. **Migration from Marketplaces to White-Label Platforms**  
   Creators are leaving Hotmart, Udemy, and Coursera for their own platforms to maintain audience control and eliminate competition from other products at checkout.

2. **Community as Product**  
   Platforms like Skool ($99/month) show that forum + course in a single experience increases student LTV by 3–4×.

3. **Mobile-First is Mandatory**  
   73% of online course accesses in 2025 were via mobile. Platforms without native app functionality lose engagement.

4. **AI Personalization**  
   Next-module recommendations, automatic quiz generation, multilingual captions (Mux Robots, etc.) are expected by students in 2026.

5. **Installment Payments (US Market)**  
   BNPL (Buy Now Pay Later) grew 57% in the US in 2025. Platforms without installment options lose conversion, especially for courses above $100.

## Hotmart — Detailed Analysis

### What is Hotmart?

Hotmart is the largest digital product marketplace in Latin America, founded in 2011 in Belo Horizonte, Brazil. It functions as both marketplace and all-in-one hosting platform.

**Current data:**

- 26 million registered users
- 370,000 registered products
- Sales in 185+ countries
- Revenue model: **9.9% of every transaction**

### Hotmart Fee Structure (2026)

| Fee Type                      | Amount                  |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Per-sale commission           | **9.9% of gross value** |
| Card installments (for buyer) | +2.49% per installment  |
| Early withdrawal              | Variable fee            |
| Monthly subscription          | $0 / month              |
| Setup fee                     | $0                      |

### Real Financial Impact for the Client

Assuming $10,000/month in gross sales:

| Scenario      | Gross Revenue | Hotmart Fee (9.9%) | Net Revenue |
| ------------- | ------------- | ------------------ | ----------- |
| $5,000/month  | $5,000        | **$495**           | $4,505      |
| $10,000/month | $10,000       | **$990**           | $9,010      |
| $20,000/month | $20,000       | **$1,980**         | $18,020     |
| $50,000/month | $50,000       | **$4,950**         | $45,050     |

**Annual (at $10K/month):** $11,880 paid to Hotmart per year.

### Hotmart Limitations for Our Client

1. **Brand Dilution:** Hotmart checkout, Hotmart app — doesn't reinforce the client's brand
2. **Competitor exposure in checkout:** Suggestions for similar products in the purchase environment
3. **Limited data control:** Difficult to export student list and integrate with own CRM
4. **Generic experience:** Impossible to visually differentiate the learning experience
5. **No integrated community:** Hotmart Club has basic forums, no groups/live integration
6. **Platform dependency:** Risk of rule changes or fee increases
7. **No native US installments:** US-market installments via Hotmart are not intuitive for American cards
