# 03 — Competitive Differentials

**Date:** 2026-04-24

---

## What Creators Hate About Hotmart

Based on market research and public feedback from Brazilian creators:

1. **"Hotmart appears alongside my product"** — Hotmart logo on emails, Hotmart app, Hotmart URL. Dilutes the creator's brand.

2. **"I pay 9.9% even when using my own audience"** — The fee is charged regardless of how the sale was generated.

3. **"My student data belongs to Hotmart, not me"** — Limited export, impossible to migrate easily.

4. **"Checkout suggests competitor products"** — Hotmart's purchase environment exposes other courses in the same niche.

5. **"Student experience is generic"** — Impossible to customize the learning experience with own brand identity.

6. **"Support is slow"** — Responses take days, not hours.

7. **"No control over mobile experience"** — Hotmart's app, not the creator's.

---

## YBR Platform Differentials

### Differential 1: 100% Branded Experience (White-Label)

**Hotmart:** Student buys at `hotmart.com/product/...`, accesses via Hotmart app, emails come from `hotmart.com`.

**YBR Platform:** Student buys at `courses.mybrand.com`, emails come from `contact@mybrand.com`, 100% creator-branded experience.

**Impact:** Increased perceived professionalism drives higher conversion and LTV. The creator is the brand, not the platform.

---

### Differential 2: Fixed Cost = More Money for the Creator

**Hotmart:** 9.9% of $10,000 = $990 paid to the platform per month.

**YBR Platform:** $99/month fixed (Growth) + 2.9% Stripe = ~$389/month for $10K.  
**Savings: $601/month, $7,212/year.**

> "You pay $99 per month, fixed. Plus 2.9% card processing fee (that's Stripe, not us). With Hotmart, you pay 9.9% of every sale. If you sell $10,000 in a month, you send $990 to Hotmart. With us, it's $99 + ~$290 = $389. You keep $601 more every month. That's $7,200 per year staying in your pocket."

---

### Differential 3: Integrated Community (Killer Feature)

**The current Hotmart problem:** Students watch lessons and disappear. No sense of community.

**What's working in 2026:** Platforms with integrated community have 3–4× higher LTV (Skool, Circle data).

**Our solution:**

- Discussion forum per module (students helping students)
- Monthly instructor live sessions integrated into the player
- WhatsApp groups with automatic integration (student purchases → automatically joins group via WhatsApp Business API)
- Q&A space answered by the instructor

---

- Native push notifications

- Fully integrated with platform system
